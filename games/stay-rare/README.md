# Stay Rare

A survivor-like for your Rare Friend: move, your weapons fire on their own, survive endless waves of 1-bit
glitches and a boss every ~90 seconds, and level up through the "Level up!" terminal (pick 1 of 3 power chips).

- Controls: WASD / arrows or drag anywhere (touch joystick). P / Esc or `[ II ]` pauses. M mutes.
- Your Friend's family decides its starting weapon and trait, and three traits read from its own pixels (Bulk, Symmetry,
  Eyes) tune it further; max a weapon with its paired passive to evolve it. Chiptune music changes when a boss arrives.
- Scenery is solid; crates and terminals break and may drop a health patch.
- Each run costs 1 simulated RF ("Run"). On death a chest opens: 99% "Pool entry", 1% "Free run" (1 RF back),
  regardless of your score.
- Economy design (simulated, not in FriendSDK v0.1.2): 0.89 RF to a Daily Pool shared by the top 10 at 00:00 UTC,
  0.01 RF free-run chest, 0.05 RF house, 0.05 RF burned.
- Fonts: Silkscreen and Archivo (SIL Open Font License, see `fonts/`). Friend artwork and props: FriendSDK (see its NOTICE.md).
